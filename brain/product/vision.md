# Product Vision

## Purpose
This file captures the product mission, target users, business model, and feature direction.

## How To Use
- Update when product positioning or priorities change.
- Keep user and business value explicit.

## Vision
Build the operating system for real-estate companies: one platform to manage operations, teams, listings, leads, clients, websites, payments, and AI-assisted workflows.

## Target Users
- Real-estate companies
- Property developers
- Estate managers
- Property agents

## Business Model
- Multi-tenant SaaS
- Monthly subscriptions
- Three launch tiers: Launch, Growth, Scale
- Internal tier keys remain `starter`, `plus`, and `pro`; customer-facing UI and docs should use Launch, Growth, and Scale.
- Additional monetization from AI credits, paid template purchases, mobile-app requests, and domain-related revenue

## Pricing Plan Direction
- Launch
- NGN 20,000 / month
- Basic website presence for smaller operators and spin-off brands
- No customer account system
- Newsletter capture available
- Subscription surfaces available
- Listings available, but no direct on-site purchase flow
- Limited to launch-tier templates
- No free launch plan is currently intended
- Growth
- Includes customer accounts
- Includes website payments and customer estate-management experiences
- Includes custom domain connection
- Includes growth-tier templates
- Scale
- Includes all Growth capabilities
- Adds AI-powered features
- Includes scale-tier templates

## Template Plan Direction
- Launch templates
- Any template marked `starter` is available for every subscription tier
- Growth templates
- Growth tenants can pick 3 growth templates for free
- Growth tenants can preview and then buy additional growth templates
- Scale tenants can access all growth templates
- Scale templates
- Scale tenants can pick 3 scale templates for free
- Scale tenants can preview and then buy additional scale templates
- Mobile app request is positioned as a scale-level template/package option

## Core User Outcomes
- Launch a professional website in minutes
- Manage properties and agents from one dashboard
- Capture and convert leads faster
- Track clients, appointments, and deal progress
- Use AI for content generation, recommendations, and workflow acceleration

## Core Platform Modules
- Company management
- Construction project management
- Property listings
- Agent management
- Client CRM
- Customer project interface
- Template-led website launch
- Property media
- Lead management
- Appointments
- Payments
- Analytics

## Platform Differentiators
- Multi-tenant website launch built into the core product
- Curated templates with editable copy and structured preview controls
- AI content generation mapped directly into section configs
- AI usage billing with tenant-level credit controls
- Domain purchase and connection inside the product
